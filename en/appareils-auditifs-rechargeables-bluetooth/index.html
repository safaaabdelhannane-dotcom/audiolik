Our solutions

Rechargeable and connected

Two developments changed daily life more than any improvement in sound: recharging and wireless connection.

No more batteries

You put the devices in their case in the evening and take them back in the morning. No tiny battery to change every few days, no failure at the wrong moment, no small waste.

For fingers that tremble a little or eyesight that is fading, this single change is often enough to make a device genuinely usable day to day.

Phone and television, straight into your ears

Calls arrive in both devices at once, without going through the phone's speaker. So does television sound, through a small transmitter — which settles at a stroke the most common argument in the living room: the volume.

Some models are controlled from an app, to adjust volume discreetly or switch programme at a restaurant.

A few limits worth knowing

A battery has a lifespan, like a phone's: after a few years, run time drops and the battery is replaced in the workshop. And a rechargeable device left out of its dock is an empty device the next day — that is a habit to build.

What people ask us most.

How long does one charge last?

In practice, a full day, streaming included. One night on the dock is enough. Exact figures depend on the model and are given to you at the centre.

Do I need a recent phone?

Not necessarily, but the simplest connection comes with a phone from recent years. We check compatibility with yours before any choice.
